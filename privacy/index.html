Privacy policy

Effective October 30, 2026

Pebblebell is a meditation app. This policy explains what it does with your information. The short version: nothing leaves your phone unless you send it.

What Pebblebell collects

Nothing. Pebblebell has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no third-party tracking code. It doesn't even have permission to use the internet, so it can't send anything anywhere.

What stays on your phone

To work, Pebblebell keeps a few things on your device:

  • Your sessions: when each sit, breathing session, or guided practice happened and how long it lasted, so the Your practice tab can show your week.
  • Your notes: anything you write in the notes on the Your practice tab.
  • Your settings: your sit length and bells, voice, colors, reminder time, and progress through Your first week.

Only you can see these. Pebblebell's developer can't see or access them.

Exporting your notes

You can export your notes to a PDF or Word file. Pebblebell asks Android where to save it, and you choose: your phone's storage, or an app like Google Drive. Once saved, the file is yours, and where it goes from there is up to you and the app you saved it to. Pebblebell never exports anything on its own.

Backups

If you've turned on Android's backup for your phone, Android may include Pebblebell's data in that backup, so your history and notes come back when you set up a new phone. Those backups are made and stored by Google under your Google account, encrypted, and are governed by Google's privacy policy. Pebblebell's developer can't access them. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Pebblebell deletes everything it stored on your phone. You can also delete it without uninstalling: Settings > Apps > Pebblebell > Storage > Clear storage. Delete a single note from the note itself.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show the session in progress (with Pause and End), pause bells, and your daily reminder, if you turn them on.
  • Foreground service and wake lock: to keep the timer and bells running on time with the screen off.
  • Vibration: for the vibrate cue and breathing guide.

Testing Pebblebell

If you join Pebblebell's test, Google Play and Google Groups share your Google account's email address with the developer, so you can be let into the test. It's used for nothing else. Leave the group, or leave the test from Pebblebell's page in Google Play, at any time.

Children

Pebblebell doesn't collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Because Pebblebell can't use the internet, a change could only arrive with an app update that you choose to install.
