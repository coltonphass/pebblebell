Try Pebblebell early

Thank you for helping. Pebblebell is a calm meditation app for Android, and it needs a few weeks of testing before Google Play lets it launch. Three steps, about a minute.

Do these on your Android phone, signed in to the same Google account you use for the Play Store.

  1. Join the testers group

    Tap Join group. You won't get any emails from it; it's only the guest list.

    Join group

  2. Accept the invite

    Tap Become a tester on the page that opens. If it says the app isn't available yet, wait a few minutes after joining the group and try again.

    Become a tester

  3. Install it

    Get Pebblebell from Google Play. Updates arrive on their own, like any other app.

    Open in Google Play

Please keep it installed

Google counts how many testers keep the app for 14 days, so leaving it on your phone for two weeks helps most, even if you only open it now and then.

Tell us what you think

Anything helps: what felt calming, what felt confusing, a bell that was too loud. Leave feedback from Pebblebell's page in Google Play (testers get a private feedback box there), or email pebblebell26@gmail.com.

Your privacy

Pebblebell has no accounts and no analytics, and it can't use the internet. Joining the test shares only your Google account's email with the developer, through Google Play's tester list. Read the privacy policy.
